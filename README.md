## Project Overview

This project analyses the sales and stock records of Wanjiku, a fresh-produce stall owner at Gikomba Market. The analysis covers 14 days of daily sales and stock information for five products. Its purpose is to evaluate business performance, identify sales trends, compare weekday and weekend performance, determine products requiring restocking and calculate gross profit margins. Core Python is used to generate insights and provide practical business recommendations.

## Top Three Findings

1. **Total Sales:** KES 46,450 over 14 days, with average daily sales of KES 3,317.86.
2. **Weekly Growth:** Sales increased from KES 22,050 in Week 1 to KES 24,400 in Week 2, representing 10.66% growth.
3. **Weekend Performance:** Average weekend sales were KES 4,900 compared to weekday average sales of KES 2,685. Tomatoes and sukuma also require restocking.

## Conclusion

The analysis indicates that Wanjiku's business experienced positive sales growth, with stronger sales performance during weekends. The findings can help improve stock management, purchasing decisions and overall business performance.
